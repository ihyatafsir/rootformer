ROOT زحلف   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root زحلف is the act of sliding or slipping downward, specifically the smooth, frictionless descent over a surface, as exemplified by children sliding from the top of a hill to its bottom. This core image of downward slippage underlies all subsequent literal and figurative applications.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah (delineated from Lisān al-ʿArab) is the trace or track left by children sliding down a hill, and by extension, the act of sliding itself.
