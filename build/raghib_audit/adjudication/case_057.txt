ROOT علا   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root is vertical elevation and height above a baseline, conceived as the spatial opposite of lowness (sifl). It denotes the act of rising, ascending, or being positioned above something, whether in physical space or in rank.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning is height, elevation, or the upper part of a thing. It is used primarily for spatial and corporeal elevation, as in 'he rose above the place' or 'the upper part of the house' (ʿilw al-dār). The verb ʿalā (يَعْلُو) is used for both praiseworthy and blameworthy elevation, whereas ʿaliya (يَعْلَى) is reserved for praiseworthy elevation only.
REC canon_def.ar     : العُلُوُّ: ضِدُّ السُّفْلِ، والارتفاعُ. عَلا يَعْلُو عُلُوًّا فهو عالٍ، وعَلِيَ يَعْلَى عَلَاءً فهو عَلِيٌّ. وعُلْوُ كلِّ شيءٍ وعِلْوُه وعَلْوُه وعُلاوَتُه وعالِيه وعالِيَتُه: أَرْفَعُه.
REC canon_def.en     : Elevation is the opposite of lowness, and it is rising. He rose, rising, and is high; he was high, being high, and is exalted. The height of anything, its upper part, its summit, and its topmost portion: its highest part.
