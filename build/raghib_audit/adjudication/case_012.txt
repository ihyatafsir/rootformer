ROOT رهط   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The root denotes a compact, tightly bound group of men — a small, cohesive band whose members are united by kinship, tribal affiliation, or a shared cause. Its foundational physical image is that of a knot or tight cluster of individuals standing together, neither a single person nor a large multitude.
REC haqiqah_literal  : A man's immediate kin-group or clan; a small company of men numbering from three up to ten (some say seven to ten). It is the concrete, bounded social unit — the band of close male relatives or associates who stand together as one body.
REC canon_def.ar     : الرَّهْطُ: قَوْمُ الرَّجُلِ وَقَبِيلَتُهُ، وَهُمْ عَدَدٌ يَجْمَعُ مِنْ ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَى عَشَرَةٍ، وَمَا دُونَ السَّبْعَةِ إِلَى الثَّلَاثَةِ نَفَرٌ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-rahṭ: a man's people and his tribe; a number gathering from three to ten; what is below seven down to three is termed nafar.
