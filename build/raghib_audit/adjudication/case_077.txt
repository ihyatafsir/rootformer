ROOT دعص   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root د ع ص is a compact, aggregated mound of sand—a small, clustered hillock of loose terrain that accumulates into a distinct elevation, smaller in scale than a large dune (ḥiqf).
REC haqiqah_literal  : A literal, physical mound or hillock of sand gathered together; a small sand-hill formed by the aggregation of loose grains, less in magnitude than a ḥiqf (large dune). The singular portion or unit of such a mound is termed a diʿṣah.
REC canon_def.ar     : الدِّعْصُ: قُورٌ مِنَ الرَّمْلِ مُجْتَمِعٌ، وَالْجَمْعُ أَدْعَاصٌ وَدِعَصَةٌ، وَهُوَ أَقَلُّ مِنَ الْحِقْفِ، وَالطَّائِفَةُ مِنْهُ دِعْصَةٌ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-diʿṣ: a compact mound of sand gathered together; the plural is adʿāṣ and diʿaṣah. It is smaller than a ḥiqf (large dune), and a single portion of it is a diʿṣah.
