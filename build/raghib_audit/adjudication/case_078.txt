ROOT دهن   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root د-ه-ن is the act of moistening, coating, or anointing a surface with a fatty, oily substance, thereby producing a slight, superficial wetness or glaze. This concrete sense of applying oil to the head or body underlies all subsequent literal and figurative extensions.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the application of oil or grease to the head or another surface (دهن رأسه، ودهنه، وادهن، وتدهن), as well as the oil itself (الدُّهْن) and the vessel that contains it (مدهن). It also denotes a tract of sandy ground (الدهناء) and a place where a small amount of water collects (مدهن).
REC canon_def.ar     : الدُّهْن: معروف. دَهَن رأْسه وغيره يَدْهُنه دَهْناً: بلَّه، والاسم الدُّهْن، والجمع أَدْهان ودِهان. والمِدْهَن: ما يجعل فيه الدهن. والدَّهين: الناقة القليلة اللبن. ودهن المطر الأرض: بلها بللاً يسيراً.
