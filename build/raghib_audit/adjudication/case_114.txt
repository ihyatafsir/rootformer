ROOT وخم   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root وخم centers on heaviness, oppressiveness, and noxiousness—specifically, that which weighs heavily upon the body or spirit, or a land/pasture that is unwholesome and burdensome to those who consume from it. The root's primary sensory anchor is the experience of something being heavy, difficult to digest, or injurious to well-being.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning denotes something that is heavy, unwholesome, or noxious—whether a person of heavy, burdensome disposition (وَخِم, وَخِيم), or a pasture (كَلأ) that is unwholesome and burdensome to livestock (مُتَوَخَّم).
