ROOT سنن   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root سنن is the tooth (al-sinn) as a hard, projecting, pointed structure—an instrument of cutting, grinding, and piercing—from which derive the senses of sharpening, pointing, flowing smoothly, and establishing a continuous, well-trodden path or normative practice.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah centers on the tooth (al-sinn) and its concrete derivatives: the sharpening of iron (sanna al-ḥadīda), the easy pouring of water (sanna al-māʾa ʿalā wajhihi), the smooth, swift movement of a horse (istanna al-faras), and the clear, beaten track of a road (sunan al-ṭarīq).
REC canon_def.ar     : السِّنُّ: واحدة الأَسنان، وهي الضِّرْسُ، أُنْثَى. والسُّنَّةُ: الطريقةُ والسيرةُ، حسنةً كانت أو قبيحةً. وسَنَّ الماءَ على وجهه: صبَّه صبًّا سهلاً. وسَنَّ الحديدةَ: حدَّدها.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-sinn: the singular of teeth, namely a molar tooth, feminine. Al-sunna: a path or mode of conduct, whether good or evil. Sanna al-māʾa ʿalā wajhihi: he poured the water out smoothly. Sanna al-ḥadīda: he sharpened the iron.
