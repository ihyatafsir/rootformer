ROOT وهن   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the diminution of structural integrity or vital force in a living body or solid object, specifically the loss of load-bearing capacity in bone, limb, or constitution, such that the entity can no longer sustain effort or resist pressure.
REC haqiqah_literal  : Literal physical weakness or enervation in bodily structure, action, or resolve; the actual slackening of bone and sinew, as in 'the bone within me has weakened' (Q 19:4), and the palpable loss of strength in a person or animal that renders them incapable of rising or exerting force.
