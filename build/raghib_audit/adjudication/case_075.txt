ROOT خضب   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root خ-ض-ب is the act of dyeing or staining a surface—specifically human hair or skin—with a coloring substance such as henna (ḥinnāʾ) or katam, thereby altering its visible hue toward red, yellow, or another color. The root's primary semantic nucleus is the material application of pigment to a corporeal substrate, producing a perceptible chromatic change.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal (ḥaqīqah) meaning is the direct, physical application of dye (khiḍāb) to hair or hand, as in 'khaḍaba shaʿrahu wa yadahu bi-l-khiḍāb' (he dyed his hair and hand with dye), yielding 'kaffin khaḍīb' (a dyed palm) and 'banānun mukhaḍḍab' (dyed fingertips).
