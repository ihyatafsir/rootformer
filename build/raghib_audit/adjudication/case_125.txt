REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the act of crushing, breaking, or violently shattering a solid body, and secondarily on the striking or bruising of a bodily part.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the crushing or shattering of something solid (al-shadkh), and the bruise or contusion (al-radakh) produced by a blow upon the body.
REC canon_def.ar     : المَرْدَخُ: الشَّدْخ. والرَّدَخُ: مثل الرَّدع، عُمانِيَّة.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-mardakh: crushing or shattering. And al-radakh: like al-radʿ (a bruise or contusion), an Omani usage.
