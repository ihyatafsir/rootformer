REC ontological_core : The foundational physical act of threading beads or pearls onto a single cord, thereby gathering discrete, scattered units into a unified, ordered sequence.
REC haqiqah_literal  : To string pearls or beads onto a thread; to arrange individual items in a linear, connected order.
REC canon_def.ar     : النَّظْمُ: التَّأْلِيفُ. نَظَمَهُ يَنْظِمُهُ نَظْمًا وَنِظَامًا، وَنَظَّمَهُ فَانْتَظَمَ وَتَنَظَّمَ. وَنَظَمْتُ اللُّؤْلُؤَ أَيْ جَمَعْتُهُ فِي السِّلْكِ.
REC canon_def.en     : Naẓm is composition. He strung it, he strings it, with naẓm and niẓām; and he organized it, so it became strung and organized. I strung the pearls, that is, I gathered them onto the cord.
