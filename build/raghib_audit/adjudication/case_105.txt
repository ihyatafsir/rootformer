ROOT نفه   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root نفه is the exhaustion and collapse of vital force, specifically the failure of the animating breath or self (nafs) under strain, and the consequent prostration of a beast of burden from fatigue.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, per Asās al-Balāghah, is the state of being exhausted, wearied, and broken by toil: a man is nāhif or manfūh (exhausted), the self (nafs) is nafihah (wearied and enfeebled), and a camel is nāfih (prostrate from fatigue), with the plural nuffah.
REC canon_def.ar     : نَفِهَتْ نفسي: أَعْيَتْ وكَلَّتْ. وبعير نافِهٌ: كالٌّ مُعْيٍ، والجمع نُفَّهٌ؛ ونَفَّهَهُ: أَتعبه حتى انقطع.
REC canon_def.en     : My self became exhausted: it grew weary and enfeebled. A camel that is nāfih is weary and prostrate; the plural is nuffah. And naffahahu means: he exhausted him until he was cut off (utterly spent).
