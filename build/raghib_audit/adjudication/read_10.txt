ROOT ضوم   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is not attested in the classical lexicographical tradition as an independent radical cluster; Ibn Manẓūr records it only as a dialectal or orthographic variant of ضيم (ḍ-y-m), whose primary physical sense is the exertion of force or pressure upon a thing, resulting in injustice or wrong.

REC ontological_core : The root ط-و-غ denotes transgression, overflow, and exceeding the proper bound — the foundational physical image being that of a torrent or flood that bursts its channel and overruns its limits, hence any entity that oversteps the measure of the created order.
REC haqiqah_literal  : In its strict literal (ḥaqīqah) sense, طاغوت designates any object of worship set up alongside or instead of God — idols, the Devil, soothsayers, or human authorities who claim absolute obedience — insofar as each has transgressed (ṭaghā) the bound of creatureliness and usurped a prerogative belonging to the Divine alone.
REC canon_def.ar     : الطَّاغُوتُ: ما عُبِدَ من دون الله عز وجل، وكلُّ رَأْسٍ في الضلالِ طاغوتٌ؛ وقيل: الأَصْنامُ، وقيل: الشيطانُ، وقيل: الكَهَنةُ، وقيل: مَرَدةُ أَهل الكتاب.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ṭāghūt: that which is worshipped besides God — Mighty and Majestic — and every leader in error is a ṭāghūt; it is said to be idols, or the Devil, or soothsayers, or the rebellious ones among the People of the Book.

ROOT طيم   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the act of innate formation and molding—God's original constitution of a creature's nature, as in clay shaped by a potter. This concrete sense of kneading and shaping underlies the abstract notion of inborn disposition.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is God's creation and formation of a being according to a fixed innate constitution, as expressed in the phrase طامَهُ اللهُ على الخَير (God molded him upon good). The noun الطِّيماء denotes the resulting innate nature or natural disposition, e.g., الشِّعْر مِنْ طِيمائِه (poetry is from his innate disposition).
REC canon_def.ar     : طامَهُ الله على الخَير يَطِيمُه طَيْماً: جَبَله. والطِّيماءُ: الجِبِلَّةُ والطبيعةُ.
REC canon_def.en     : God molded him upon good, He forms him innately: He created him with an innate constitution. And الطِّيماء means innate nature and natural disposition.
