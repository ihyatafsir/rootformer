REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the spatial extent of the two outstretched hands, i.e., the distance between the two palms when the arms are fully extended; from this concrete measure of bodily span, the root extends to the act of measuring by that span and to the stretching forth of the forelegs in a beast's stride.
