ROOT درهم   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the notion of falling or dropping down, specifically from extreme old age, yielding a state of decrepitude and collapse.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Lisān al-ʿArab is the falling or dropping of a person due to extreme old age; the adjective al-mudrahimm denotes one who has collapsed from senility, i.e., the very aged.
REC canon_def.ar     : المُدْرَهِمُّ: الساقطُ من الكِبَرِ، وقيل: هو الكبيرُ السِّنِّ أَيّاً كان. وقد ادْرَهَمَّ يَدْرَهِمُّ ادْرِهْمَاماً أَي سقط من الكبر.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-mudrahimm: one who has fallen from extreme old age; it is also said: he is the very aged, whoever he may be. And he has adrahamma, yadrahimmu, idrihmāman, meaning he fell from old age.
