REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root ج-ي-ش is the turbulent, surging motion of a liquid boiling and overflowing its vessel, from which the sense of a churning, agitated mass—whether of the self or of armed men—is derived.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the boiling and overflowing of a pot (جاشت القدر واستجاشت: غلت), and the consequent agitation of the soul (جاشت النفس: فاظت or غثت).
REC canon_def.ar     : جاشت القدر واستجاشت: غلت. وجاشَت النفسُ تَجِيش جَيْشاً وجُيوشاً وجَيَشاناً: فاظَتْ. وجيش فلان: جمع جيشا.
REC canon_def.en     : The pot boiled and overflowed. The soul surged with a boiling, overflowing, and agitation. A person mustered an army.
