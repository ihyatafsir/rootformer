ROOT مدن   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the act of settling and taking up residence in a place, from which the noun for a settlement or city derives. Ibn Manẓūr notes that the verb مَدَنَ بِالمَكَانِ (madana bi-l-makān) meaning 'to dwell in a place' is a dead verb (فِعْلٌ مُمَات), preserved chiefly in the derived noun المَدِينَة (al-madīnah).
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is a place of residence and settlement, i.e., a city or town. Al-Rāghib al-Iṣfahānī reports that some hold المَدِينَة to be of the pattern فَعِيلَة (faʿīlah), with its plural مَدَن (madan), while others treat the mīm as augmentative, making it مَفْعِلَة (mafʿilah) from دِنْتُ (dintu), meaning 'I was possessed or ruled.' REC canon_def.ar     : مَدَنَ بِالمَكَانِ: أَقَامَ بِهِ، فِعْلٌ مُمَات، وَمِنْهُ المَدِينَة، وَهِيَ فَعِيلَة، وَتُجْمَعُ عَلَى مَدَائِنَ وَمُدْنٍ وَمُدُنٍ.
REC canon_def.en     : مَدَنَ بِالمَكَانِ: he dwelt in the place—a dead verb—and from it derives المَدِينَة (the city), which is of the pattern فَعِيلَة, and its plurals are مَدَائِن, مُدْن, and مُدُن.
