ROOT كمع   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) denotes co-lying, the act of two bodies sharing a single resting-place or bed, and by extension the intimate companion who shares that bed.
REC haqiqah_literal  : Literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah: كَامَعَ المرأَةَ signifies he lay with the woman as a bedfellow; الكِمْع and الكَمِيع denote the bedfellow or consort.
REC canon_def.ar     : كامَعَ المرأَةَ: ضَاجَعَها، والكِمْعُ والكَمِيعُ: الضَّجِيعُ؛ ومنه قيل للزوج: هو كَمِيعُها.
REC canon_def.en     : He lay with the woman as a bedfellow; الكِمْع and الكَمِيع denote the bedfellow; hence the husband is called her كَمِيع (bedfellow).
