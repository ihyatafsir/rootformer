ROOT شبث   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root ش-ب-ث is the act of clinging, adhering, or becoming physically attached to an object, such that it is seized and held fast. The root denotes a concrete, tactile grasping or entanglement—a thing becoming caught upon or fastened to another.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is: to cling to something, to hold fast to it, and to become attached to it. The phrase تشبث به (tashabbatha bihi) denotes direct, physical clinging or adherence. The related form شابثه (shābathahu) indicates mutual clinging or grappling. REC canon_def.en     : He clung to the thing: he became attached to it and took hold of it. Clinging to a thing is attachment to it. And attachment is clinging.
