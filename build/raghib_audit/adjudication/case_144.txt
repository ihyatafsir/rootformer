ROOT مقق   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is extreme elongation combined with extreme thinness — a stretched, attenuated bodily frame, and by extension the act of drawing out or extracting something entirely from its container.
REC haqiqah_literal  : In its literal physical sense, مَقَق denotes excessive length conjoined with fineness or slenderness; a man is described as أَمَقّ and a woman as مَقَّاء, and a horse as أَشَقّ أَمَقّ. It also denotes the complete extraction of marrow from a bone (تَمَقَّقْتُ ما في العظم) and the draining of milk from the udder by a young camel (تَمَقَّقَ الفصيل ما في الضرع).
REC canon_def.ar     : المَقَقُ: الطول عامة، وقيل: هو الطول الفاحش في دقة. ورجل أمق وامرأة مقاء. وتمققت ما في العظم: استخرجته كله. وفلان مقامق: يتكلم بأقصى حلقه.
