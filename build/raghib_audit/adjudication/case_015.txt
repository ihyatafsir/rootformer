REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is spatial and relational estrangement: to be far removed, distant, or remote. This is exemplified by the long, tightly twisted rope (شَطَن) used to draw water from a deep well or to tether a horse, and by the deep well (بئر شطون) whose bottom is far from its mouth.
