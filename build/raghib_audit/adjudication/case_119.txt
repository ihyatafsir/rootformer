ROOT جنز   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin is the act of covering, concealing, or shrouding an object — a concrete operation of overlaying something so as to hide it from view.
REC haqiqah_literal  : To cover or conceal a thing; to shroud it. The verb جَنَزَ الشيءَ يَجْنِزُه جَنْزاً denotes the literal physical act of draping or wrapping an entity so that it is hidden.
REC canon_def.ar     : جَنَزَ الشيءَ يَجْنِزُه جَنْزاً: ستره. وذكروا أَن النَّوَار لما احْتُضِرَت أَوْصَت أَن يصلي عليها الحسن، فقيل له في ذلك، فقال: إِذا جَنَزْتُموها فآذِنُوني.
