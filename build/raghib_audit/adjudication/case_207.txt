ROOT ضخخ   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root ضخخ is the forceful, extended emission of a liquid stream—specifically urine or water—from an orifice or conduit, characterized by propulsion and continuity rather than mere spillage.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the extension and projection of urine (امتداد البول), and by extension the forceful ejection of water through a tube or pipe; the instrument المضخة is a reed or tube containing a wooden rod used to expel water from the mouth.
