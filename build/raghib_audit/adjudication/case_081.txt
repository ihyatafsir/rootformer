ROOT رضم   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root ر ض م is the act of stacking large, heavy stones one upon another to form a solid, compacted structure or barrier. This core image of piling and compacting heavy solids underlies all subsequent literal and figurative applications.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the placing of stones one above another in building, and the resulting structure of stacked large rocks. It denotes both the action of stacking stones (رضم الحجارة) and the object itself: a wall or building constructed of dry-stone masonry (رضيم), or a heap of large rocks (رضمة).
