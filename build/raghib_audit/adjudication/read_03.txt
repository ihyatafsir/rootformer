ROOT بصق   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the forceful expulsion of saliva or spittle from the mouth, a physiological act of ejection. This root is a phonetic variant of ب ز ق (b-z-q) and ب س ق (b-s-q), all sharing the core sense of emitting moisture or matter outward.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is to spit or expectorate saliva from the mouth. In Asās al-Balāghah, it is used literally in the phrase 'بصق في وجهه' (he spat in his face) as an act of contempt. It also denotes a white, glistening stone, termed 'بُصَاقَة القمر' (the moon's spittle), due to its resemblance to expelled saliva in whiteness and luminosity.
REC canon_def.ar     : البُصاقُ: لغة في البُزاق، بَصَق يَبْصُق بَصْقاً. وبُصاقةُ القمر وبُصاقُه: حجر أبيض مُتَلأْلئٌ. وبُصاقُ الإبل: نبت.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Buṣāq: a variant of al-buzāq (spittle); baṣaqa yabṣuqu baṣqan (he spat, spitting). And the moon's spittle (buṣāqat al-qamar / buṣāquhu): a white, glistening stone. And the spittle of camels (buṣāq al-ibil): a plant.
