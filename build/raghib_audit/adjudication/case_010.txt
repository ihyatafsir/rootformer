ROOT دخل   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root is the act of entering or penetrating into an interior space, conceived as the direct antonym of exiting (خروج). It denotes the crossing of a boundary from outside to inside, whether spatial, temporal, or functional.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the act of entering a place, as in 'he entered such-and-such a place.' It also concretely denotes the inner lining of a garment that touches the body (داخلة الإزار), the interlocking joints of armor (حلق الدرع مداخل), and the inner, hidden reality of a person's affairs (دخلة الأمر).
