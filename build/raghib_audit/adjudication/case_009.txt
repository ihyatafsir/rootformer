REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root is the act of covering or being covered by an outer garment (dithār) that is worn over the inner garment (shīʿār), thereby concealing what lies beneath. From this primary sense of overlaying and concealment, the root extends to the notion of something being overlaid by time, dust, or neglect, leading to its effacement and decay.
