ROOT همي   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root همي is the free, downward, and continuous flow of a liquid—specifically tears, rain, or blood—from a source, and by extension, the swift, unresisted departure or movement of a being or object.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the pouring forth and flowing of tears from the eye, rain from the sky, or blood from the mouth of a horse; also, the going away or departing of camels upon their faces.
REC canon_def.ar     : هَمَتْ عينُه هَمْياً وهُمِيًّا وهَمَياناً: صَبَّتْ دمعها، وقيل: سالَ دَمْعُها، وكذلك كلُّ سائل من مطر وغيره. وليس هذا من الهائم في شيء.
REC canon_def.en     : His eye flowed with tears: it poured forth its tears; it was also said: its tears streamed. The same applies to any flowing liquid such as rain and the like. This is not related to the wandering lover (al-hāʾim) in any way.
