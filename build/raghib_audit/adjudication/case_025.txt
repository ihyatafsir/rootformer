REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root is the act of something separating, detaching, or falling away from a larger body or surface—such as hair, fur, or feathers shedding from an animal, or a garment slipping off a person. This core image of detachment and outward emission underlies all subsequent derivations.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning is the falling or shedding of hair, fur, or feathers from an animal, or the slipping off of a garment from a person. It denotes a concrete, observable separation of a part from its whole.
