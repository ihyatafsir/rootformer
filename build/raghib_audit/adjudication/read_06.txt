REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the act of defecating or excreting, as in ضَفَعَ الرجلُ (the man defecated). It also extends to the emission of urine and dung, as in ضَفَعَ بِبَوْلِهِ وَسَلَحَ (he urinated and defecated).

ROOT مكد   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the notion of fixed, unmoving persistence in a place or state — a thing that stays put and does not shift, flow away, or dissipate. From this concrete sense of stationary continuance, the root extends to denote anything enduring, perpetual, or unceasing, such as water that remains and does not dry up.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is: to remain, stay, abide, or dwell in a place (مَكَدَ بالمكان يَمْكُدُ مُكُوداً: أَقام به). It is synonymous with ثَكَمَ (to stay) and رَكَدَ (to be still, motionless). Applied to water, ماءٌ ماكِدٌ denotes water that is permanent, unfailing, and never ceasing — standing water that persists rather than draining or evaporating.
REC canon_def.ar     : مَكَدَ بالمكان يَمْكُدُ مُكُوداً: أَقام به؛ وثَكَمَ يَثْكُم مثله، ورَكَدَ رُكوداً. وماءٌ ماكِدٌ: دائمٌ.
REC canon_def.en     : مَكَدَ in a place, يَمْكُدُ مُكُوداً: he stayed or dwelt there; ثَكَمَ يَثْكُم is the same, and رَكَدَ رُكوداً [is likewise]. And ماءٌ ماكِدٌ means perpetual, unfailing water.
