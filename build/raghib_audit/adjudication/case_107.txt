REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the act of directing oneself toward a place or object with deliberate intent, specifically the setting of one's course toward a destination or the resolve to undertake a journey. It denotes the orienting of the self toward a goal, whether spatial (a dwelling, a land) or volitional (an intention, a belief).
