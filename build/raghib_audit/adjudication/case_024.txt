ROOT مقت   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the intense, visceral aversion aroused by the perception of something ugly or morally repugnant; it denotes a hatred that is not merely emotional but is provoked by the apprehension of a blameworthy act or quality.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal meaning is to hate someone intensely because of a vile or repulsive matter; it is a loathing directed at a person due to an ugly deed, and it is used literally in expressions such as 'he hated him with intense hatred' and 'he became hateful to people'.
