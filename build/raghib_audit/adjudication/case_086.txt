ROOT طلس   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root ط ل س is the act of effacing, obliterating, or wiping out written traces from a surface, and the resulting state of a document or hide whose markings have been erased or obscured. From this primary sense of erasure and obscuration, the root extends to denote a dusty, ash-grey, or dusky coloration that obscures the natural brightness of a thing, as in the grey wolf (ذئب أطلس).
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the effacement of writing: طَلَسَ الكتابَ means to erase the book, and طَلَّسَهُ means to efface it thoroughly so as to corrupt its script. When the effacement is complete and the writing is reduced to a blank, superfluous surface, it becomes a طِرْس. The physical instrument of erasure is the طَلَاسَة, a rag or cloth […]
