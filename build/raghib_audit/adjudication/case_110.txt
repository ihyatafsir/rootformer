ROOT هوش   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root ه و ش is the scattering, dispersal, and agitated commotion of a herd or group—specifically camels or horses—when startled, causing them to break ranks, mill about, and intermix chaotically. From this primary image of frightened, dispersing animals, the root extends to any state of disorderly mixing, upheaval, or collective agitation.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, as attested in Asās al-Balāghah, is the startled scattering and restless milling of camels or horses in a raid or alarm (هاشت الإبل هوشاً: نفرت في الغارة فتبددت وتفرقت); and the physical act of mixing or gathering something from here and there (هاش الشيء وهوشه: خلطه وجمعه من هنا وهنا).
