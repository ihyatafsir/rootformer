ROOT فرم   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root فرم is the act of constricting, narrowing, or tightening an orifice—specifically the vagina—by means of a medicinal agent or physical obstruction, thereby inducing a state of constriction (taḍayyuq).
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is the use of a drug or medicinal substance (farām) to constrict the vagina, as in the phrase 'istaframat al-marʾah' (the woman used a constricting agent). The root also appears in the proverbial expression 'more abject than the faram of a slave-girl,' referring to the medicinal plug used for vaginal constriction.
REC canon_def.ar     : الفَرْمُ والفِرَامُ: ما تَتَضَيَّقُ به المرأَة من دواء. وامرأَةٌ فَرْماءُ ومُسْتَفْرِمة: وهي التي تجعل الدواء في فرجها ليضيق.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-farm and al-firām: a medicinal substance by which a woman constricts [her vagina]. A woman who is farmāʾ or mustafrimah is one who places a drug in her vulva in order to narrow it.
