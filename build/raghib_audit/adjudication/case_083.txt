ROOT سحم   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root سحم is the quality of deep, uniform blackness, specifically the saturated darkness exemplified by the raven's plumage and heavy rain-clouds; it denotes a blackness that is intrinsic and total rather than accidental or partial.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is blackness (al-sawād), as in the phrase 'a raven that is asḥam' (ghurāb asḥam), 'black clouds' (saḥāb asḥam), and 'a black cloud-mass' (ghamāma saḥmāʾ); the verbal form saḥamū is used literally for darkening or blackening a face.
REC canon_def.ar     : السَّحَمُ والسُّحَامُ والسُّحْمَةُ: السواد، وكل أسود أسحم.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-saḥam, al-suḥām, and al-suḥma denote blackness; everything black is asḥam.
