ROOT قشر   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical act of stripping away an outer covering from a thing, thereby exposing what lies beneath; the root denotes the removal of a protective or concealing integument, whether by peeling, flaying, or husking.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical removal of an outer layer or rind from a thing, such as peeling a fruit or stripping bark from a tree; the integument itself, especially the rind or husk of fruit, the skin of a snake, or the outer covering of a garment.
