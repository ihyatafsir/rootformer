ROOT وخط   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the act of striking or piercing from a distance with a pointed instrument (spear, sword), and by extension, the even intermingling or spreading of a quality across a surface, such as the dispersal of grayness (shayb) through the hair.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is to strike or pierce someone from afar with a spear or sword (وخطه بالرمح، ووخطته بالسيف: تناولته به من بعيد). It also denotes the wide stride of an ostrich (مر الظليم يخط وخطا وهو سعة خطوه).
REC canon_def.ar     : الوَخْطُ: الطَّعْنُ من بعيد، ومنه وخْطُ الشيبِ الرأسَ: مخالطته إياه، والوَخْطُ من القَتِير: النَّبْذُ، وقيل: استواء البياض والسواد.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Wakhṭ: striking or piercing from a distance; hence the intermingling of gray hair with the head, and al-wakhṭ from dust: dispersal, or the equal balance of whiteness and blackness.
