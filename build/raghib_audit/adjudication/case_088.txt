ROOT فخذ   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical referent is the anatomical segment of the limb situated between the hip and the knee—the thigh—conceived as a distinct, bounded bodily member whose primary ontological mode is that of a corporeal part susceptible to injury.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, strictly anatomical sense is the thigh: the bodily member extending from the hip joint to the knee. In its verbal derivation, the root denotes the infliction of injury upon this member, as in 'he struck his thigh, rendering it fractured' (كُسِرَتْ فَخِذُهُ فَهُوَ مَفْخُوذٌ), and 'I struck him so that I injured his thigh' (رَمَيْتُهُ فَفَخَذْتُهُ).
REC canon_def.ar     : الفَخِذُ: وصل ما بين الساق والورك، أُنثى، والجمع أَفخاذ. وفُخِذَ فَخْذاً، فهو مفخوذ: أُصيبت فخذه.
REC canon_def.en     : The fakhidh is the anatomical segment connecting the lower leg and the hip; it is feminine, and its plural is afkhādh. One is said to have been fakhidha—sustaining a fakhdh—when their thigh has been injured, rendering them mafkhūdh.
