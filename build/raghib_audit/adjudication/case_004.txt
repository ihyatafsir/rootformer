ROOT بصل   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical referent is the edible bulb of the onion plant (Allium cepa), a layered, subterranean vegetable whose concentric sheaths constitute its primary concrete reality.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning is the well-known onion bulb, and by concrete extension, any object resembling its layered, ovoid form — most notably the iron boss or pointed cap of a helmet (bayḍat al-ḥadīd) and the iron head-piece of a mace or lance.
REC canon_def.ar     : البَصَل معروف، الواحدة بَصَلة، وتُشَبَّه به بَيْضة الحَدِيد؛ والبَصَلة إِنما هي سَفِيفة واحدة.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-baṣal is the well-known onion; the singular is baṣalah. The iron boss of a helmet is likened to it; and a baṣalah is but a single sheath or layer.
