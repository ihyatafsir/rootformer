ROOT فطن   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin centers on a sharp, penetrating cognitive grasp — an acute mental alertness that seizes upon a matter swiftly and thoroughly, as opposed to dullness or obtuseness (ghabāwah). It is the mind's capacity to apprehend subtle distinctions and respond with precision.
REC haqiqah_literal  : To be acutely aware of, to grasp a matter with mental sharpness, to perceive something with alert intelligence; said of a person who is keenly attentive and quick to understand (فَطِنٌ), and of the act of making someone else attentive or sharpening their understanding (تفطين).
REC canon_def.ar     : الفِطْنَةُ: كالفهم، وهي ضد الغباوة. ورجل فَطِنٌ بَيِّنُ الفِطْنَة والفَطَن، وقد فَطَنَ لهذا الأمر يَفْطُنُ فِطْنَةً، وفَطُنَ.
