ROOT موا   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root م و ا is a secondary, denominative formation from the primary root م و ه / م ي ه (water), denoting the substance of water and, by extension, anything that shares its defining property of limpid transparency and reflective clarity.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical referent of the derived noun مَاوِيَّة is a mirror (مِرْآة), so named because it is fashioned from, or functions as, a polished watery surface: images are seen in it exactly as they are seen in clear, still water. A second literal application is the crystal stone (حَجَر البِلَّوْر), i.e. rock crystal, on account of its water-like translucency.
REC canon_def.ar     : المَاوِيَّةُ: المِرْآةُ، كأَنَّها نُسِبَت إِلى الماءِ لصَفائها وأَنَّ الصُّورَ تُرى فيها كما تُرى في الماءِ الصافي، والميمُ أَصليةٌ فيها؛ وقيل: المَاوِيَّةُ حَجَرُ البِلَّوْرِ، وثلاثُ مَاوِيَّاتٍ.
