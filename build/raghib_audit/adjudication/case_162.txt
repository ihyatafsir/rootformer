ROOT جلنب   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the concrete image of a she-camel that is simultaneously fat and firm—an animal whose body combines abundant adipose tissue with a hard, compact, unyielding frame. This dual physical quality of corporeal density and solidity constitutes the root's primary sensory substrate, from which any further usage must be metaphorically derived.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, as attested in the classical lexical tradition, is a she-camel characterized by stoutness and hardness: ناقة جَلَنْباةٌ, i.e., a fat, firm she-camel. The term denotes a concrete, zoological referent and carries no abstract or figurative sense in its foundational usage.
