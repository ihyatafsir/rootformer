ROOT سون   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin is not attested as an independent verbal root in the classical lexica; its primary lexical datum is the toponym سُوَان (Suwān), a place-name. A secondary, dialectal usage is التَّسَوُّن (tasuwwun), denoting a slackening or relaxation of the belly, which Ibn Manẓūr and Abū Manṣūr relate to the root س و ل (s-w-l) through the substitution of the final lām with nūn.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning is twofold: (1) سُوَان, a specific place-name; (2) التَّسَوُّن, the slackening or relaxation of the belly, as transmitted from Ibn al-Aʿrābī.
REC canon_def.ar     : سُوَانُ: موضع. والتَّسَوُّنُ: استرخاء البطن، كأَنه من التَّسَوُّل من سَوِلَ يَسْوَلُ إذا استرخى، فأُبدلت اللام نوناً.
REC canon_def.en     : Suwān: a place-name. Al-tasuwwun: the slackening of the belly, as if derived from al-tasawwul from sawila yaswalu, meaning 'to become slack,' with the lām substituted by nūn.
