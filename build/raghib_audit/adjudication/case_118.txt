ROOT جلع   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the removal of a covering or barrier, specifically the abandonment of modesty and restraint, manifesting as shamelessness and indecent speech or behavior.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah (though not attested, derived from Lisān) is a woman's discarding of shame and speaking obscenely, or displaying herself provocatively.
REC canon_def.ar     : جَلِعَت المرأَةُ، بالكسر، جَلَعاً، فهي جَلِعةٌ وجالِعةٌ، وجَلَعَت وهي جالع وجالَعَت وهي مُجالِعٌ كله إذا ترَكت الحَياء وتكلمت بالقبيح، وقيل إِذا كانت متَبرِّجةً.
REC canon_def.en     : The woman became shameless (jalʿa), with a kasra, showing shamelessness (jalaʿan); she is shameless (jaliʿah) and shameless (jāliʿah). And she acted shamelessly (jalaʿat) while being shameless (jāliʿ), and she engaged in shameless behavior  […]
