REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root ن ك د is the notion of something sought or desired being obtained only with difficulty, hardship, or scarcity — a thing that resists its seeker and yields its benefit grudgingly. From this core emerges the associated sense of ill-omen, meanness, and depletion.
REC haqiqah_literal  : In its literal physical sense, ن ك د denotes a thing that comes to its seeker only with hardship and difficulty.
