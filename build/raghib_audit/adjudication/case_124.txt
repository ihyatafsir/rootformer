ROOT ذفل   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root denotes a thin, fluid, resinous substance — specifically the light, unthickened stage of tar (qaṭirān) before it congeals into the dense pitch (khaḍkhāḍ). Its foundational physical image is that of a viscous liquid exudate, prior to coagulation.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical referent is thin tar (al-qaṭirān al-raqīq) — the runny, uncondensed resinous fluid that precedes the thickened pitch known as khaḍkhāḍ.
REC canon_def.ar     : الذَّفْل والذِّفْل: القَطِران الرقيق الذي قبل الخَضْخاض.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-dhafl and al-dhifl: the thin tar that exists prior to the thickened pitch (al-khaḍkhāḍ).
