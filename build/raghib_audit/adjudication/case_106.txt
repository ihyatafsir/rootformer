ROOT نول   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the act of giving, bestowing, or reaching out something to someone, particularly a benefit or favor, and by extension, the reception or attainment of that benefit. It implies a transfer or extension from a giver to a receiver, often involving a hand or means of conveyance.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is the act of giving or handing over something, as in 'نوله' (he gave him) and 'ناوله الشيء' (he handed him the thing). It also denotes the reception of that thing, as in 'تنولته' (I received it). The literal sense involves a tangible transfer of an object or benefit from one person to another.
REC canon_def.ar     : النَّائِلُ: مَا نِلْتَ مِنْ مَعْرُوفِ إِنْسَانٍ، وَكَذَلِكَ النَّوَالُ. وَأَنَالَهُ مَعْرُوفَهُ وَنَوَّلَهُ: أَعْطَاهُ مَعْرُوفَهُ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Nāʾil: what you attain of a person's favor, and likewise al-nawāl. And anālahu maʿrūfahu wa nawwalahu: he gave him his favor.
