ROOT صرط   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The root denotes a traversed, extended path or roadway; its foundational physical image is a linear, beaten track that one follows toward a destination. Classical philologists note the phonological proximity of the voiceless alveolar sibilant /s/ and the emphatic alveolar sibilant /ṣ/, with the ṣād form being a dialectal or assimilated variant of the underlying sīn, owing to the adjacency of the emphatic ṭāʾ.
REC haqiqah_literal  : A road, path, or way; specifically, a clear and straight thoroughfare that is physically traversed. The literal sense is attested in the paired forms al-ṣirāṭ and al-sirāṭ, both denoting a roadway.
REC canon_def.ar     : الصراط: الطريق المستقيم. وقال الأزهري: وأصل صاده سين قلبت مع الطاء صاداً لقرب مخارجها. وقال الجوهري: الصراط والسراط.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ṣirāṭ: the straight path. Al-Azharī said: the original of its ṣād is a sīn, which was changed to ṣād alongside the ṭāʾ due to the proximity of their points of articulation. Al-Jawharī said: al-ṣirāṭ and al-sirāṭ.
