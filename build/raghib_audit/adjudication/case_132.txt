ROOT مسط   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root م س ط is the manual insertion of the hand into the reproductive cavity of a she-camel or mare to extract the accumulated seminal fluid (wathr) that has failed to result in conception after repeated copulation. The root thus denotes a concrete veterinary intervention: a tactile, evacuative act performed by the herdsman to cleanse the womb and restore fertility.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, as attested in Lisān al-ʿArab, is the act of a man inserting his hand into the vagina of a she-camel to extract the wathr—the retained water of the stallion that has coagulated in the womb due to excessive mounting without conception. The verb مَسَطَ النَّاقَةَ / الفَرَسَ يَمْسُطُهَا مَسْطًا signifies this direct manual extraction.
REC canon_def.ar     : المِسْطُ: أَن يُدخِل الرجُل يدَه في حَياء الناقة فيستَخْرِج وَثْرها، وهو ماء الفحل يجتمع في رحمها، وذلك إِذا كثر ضِرابُها ولم تَلْقَح. ومَسَطَ الناقَةَ والفَرَسَ يَمْسُطُها مَسْطاً: أَدخل يدَه في حَيائها فاستخرج وَثْرها.
