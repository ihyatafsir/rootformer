ROOT انك   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root denotes a state of intense, burdensome hardship or constriction, with a secondary physical sense of bending or curving under pressure.
REC haqiqah_literal  : Literal physical meaning: to be in a state of hardship, distress, or constriction; also to bend or curve.
REC canon_def.ar     : الأَنْكُ: الشِّدَّةُ وَالضِّيقُ، وَأَنِكَ الرَّجُلُ إِذَا وَقَعَ فِي شِدَّةٍ، وَأَنَكَ الشَّيْءَ إِذَا عَطَفَهُ وَحَنَاهُ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ank: hardship and constriction; a man is in hardship when he falls into distress; to bend something is to curve and flex it.
