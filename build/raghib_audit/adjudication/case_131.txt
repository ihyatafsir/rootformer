ROOT فجس   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is not attested in Al-Mufradāt; however, the Lisān material points to a core sense of swelling, rising, and towering—a physical expansion upward that manifests as haughtiness and self-magnification.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is not explicitly attested; based on Lisān al-ʿArab, the ḥaqīqah is the act of swelling, rising, and towering physically, as in the swelling of a she-camel's hump or the rising of a structure.
REC canon_def.ar     : الفَجْسُ والتَفَجُّس: عَظَمة وتَكَبّر وتطاوُل.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-fajs and al-tafajjus: greatness, pride, and self-elevation.
