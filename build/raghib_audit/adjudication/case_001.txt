REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root is the waist-wrapper or loincloth (al-izār) that a person binds around the waist as a covering garment; from this concrete act of girding and enclosing derives the sense of surrounding, supporting, and strengthening.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the izār: the garment wrapped around the waist, also called izārah and miʾzar; it denotes the act of wearing such a wrapper and the manner of girding it (al-izrah), as in the phrase 'he is excellent in his manner of wearing the izār' and 'for every Arab tribe there is an izrah by which they gird themselves.'
