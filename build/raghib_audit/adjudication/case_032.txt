ROOT ثور   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the eruptive dispersal and surging agitation of a substance — dust, cloud, or the like — rising and spreading outward in a visible, turbulent motion.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the surging, eruption, and dispersal of dust, clouds, or similar substances, rising and spreading visibly; also the agitating of the earth to bring forth its contents, and the bull (ثور) as the animal by which the earth is agitated for plowing.
REC canon_def.ar     : ثَارَ الشيءُ ثَوْراً وثُؤُوراً وثَوَرَاناً وتَثَوَّرَ: هاج وانتشر ساطعاً؛ وأَثَارَهُ غيره. والثَّوْرُ: البقر الذي يُثار به الأرض، وأصله مصدر جُعل في موضع الفاعل.
