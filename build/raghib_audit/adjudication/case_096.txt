REC ontological_core : The foundational physical act of striking or crushing the neck (دَقُّ العُنُق) with a rod until the bone fractures while the skin remains intact; by extension, the steeping or soaking of salted fish in water or vinegar to draw out its brine.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical sense is the crushing or pounding of the neck with a stick, breaking the bone beneath unbroken skin. A secondary literal sense is the immersion of salted fish in water or vinegar (نَقْع السَّمَك المَالِح) to leach out its salt.
