ROOT جير   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin is a particle of affirmation and assent, functioning as a vocal gesture of confirmation equivalent to 'yes' or 'indeed'. It is not derived from a concrete physical object but from a primitive linguistic utterance of agreement.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal meaning is the affirmative particle جَيْرِ, used to express assent, confirmation, or agreement, synonymous with أَجَلْ (yes/indeed).
REC canon_def.ar     : جَيْرِ: بمعنى أَجَلْ
REC canon_def.en     : Jayri: meaning 'yes' or 'indeed'.
