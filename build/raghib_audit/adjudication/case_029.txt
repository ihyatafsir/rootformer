ROOT وكل   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root و-ك-ل is the act of entrusting or delegating a matter to another, thereby relying upon them and making them a substitute or representative for oneself. It denotes the transfer of responsibility and the consequent dependence on the appointed agent.
REC haqiqah_literal  : In its literal, physical sense according to Asās al-Balāghah, the root signifies the act of appointing someone as a representative or guardian over a matter (وَكَّلَ إِلَيْهِ الأَمْرَ), such that the matter becomes entrusted to them (مَوْكُول إِلَيْهِ). It also denotes the state of being weak and dependent, relying on others (وَكَلٌ، وَكَلَةٌ، تُكَلَةٌ، مُوَاكِلٌ).
REC canon_def.ar     : الوَكِيلُ: هو المقيم الكفيل بأَرزاق العباد، وحقيقته أَنه يستقلُّ بأَمر المَوْكول إِليه. والتوكيل: أن تعتمد على غيرك وتجعله نائبا عنك.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Wakīl: He who independently manages the sustenance of servants; in essence, He who independently takes charge of the matter entrusted to Him. Entrusting (tawkīl) means to rely upon another and make them a substitute for yourself.
