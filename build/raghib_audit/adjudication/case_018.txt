REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the act of enclosing, sheathing, or wrapping an object within a protective or containing cover (ghilāf), such as a sword in its scabbard, a flask in its case, or a bud within its calyx.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is a thing covered by a sheath or container, e.g., a sheathed sword (sayf aghlaf), a covered saddle (raḥl maghlūf), or a covered beard dyed with henna.
