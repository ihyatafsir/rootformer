REC ontological_core : The root denotes a rare, non-productive nominal pattern (fuʿlūl) attested only in the proper name Banū Saʿfūq, a clan or tribal group in al-Yamāma; its foundational origin is onomastic rather than lexical, with no attested verbal or abstract nominal derivation.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, concrete referent is the tribal designation Banū Saʿfūq (also vocalized Banū Suʿfūq), a kinship group or client clan (khawal) settled in al-Yamāma; the word functions strictly as a proper noun and carries no physical or sensory meaning beyond this referential use.
