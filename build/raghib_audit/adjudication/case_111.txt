ROOT واب   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin centers on the somatic and affective response of shame or shyness—a visceral recoil or bashfulness that manifests in the body and demeanor, particularly in the context of social exposure or moral transgression.
REC haqiqah_literal  : To feel shame, to be shy, to be bashful; the literal physical manifestation of shame as a recoiling or shrinking away from exposure.
REC canon_def.ar     : الوَبْءُ: الحَيَاءُ والاسْتِحْيَاءُ. وَبِئَ يَوْبَأُ وَبْئًا ووَبَاءً: اسْتَحْيَا. وَمَا بِكَ فِي هَذَا إِبَةٌ أَيْ مَا بِكَ حَيَاءٌ. وَطَعَامٌ لَا تُؤْبَةَ فِيهِ أَيْ لَا يُسْتَحْيَا مِنْ أَكْلِهِ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-wabʾ is shame and shyness. Wabiʾa yawbaʾu wabʾan wa-wabāʾan: he felt shame. 'What is there in this for you of shame?' means 'you have no shame in this.' And 'food in which there is no shame' means 'food that is not shameful to eat.'
