ROOT ذوب   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical process of a solid substance losing its rigidity and flowing, i.e., the liquefaction of fat, snow, or any congealed body through the application of heat or an external agent.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, sensory meaning: the melting or dissolving of a solid (such as fat, snow, or ice) into a liquid state, and the transitive act of causing such melting.
REC canon_def.ar     : الذَّوْبُ: ضِدُّ الجُمُودِ. ذَابَ يَذُوبُ ذَوْبًا وذَوَبَانًا: نَقِيضُ جَمَدَ. وأَذَابَهُ غَيْرُهُ، وأَذَبْتُهُ، وذَوَّبْتُهُ، واسْتَذَبْتُهُ: طَلَبْتُ مِنْهُ ذَاكَ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-dhawb is the opposite of solidification. Dhāba yadhūbu dhawban wa-dhawabānan: it is the contrary of jamada (to freeze). Another agent may cause it to melt: adhābahu, adhabtuhu, dhawwabtuhu, and istadhabtuhu mean 'I sought that from it.'
