ROOT قسر   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root is the act of overpowering and subduing another by force, compelling them against their will.
REC haqiqah_literal  : To overpower, subdue, or compel someone to do something against their will; to coerce or force.
REC canon_def.ar     : القَسْرُ: القَهْرُ على الكُرْه. قَسَرَه يَقْسِرُه قَسْراً واقْتَسَرَه: غَلَبه وقَهَره، وقَسَرَه على الأَمر قَسْراً: أَكرهه عليه.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-qasr is overpowering by force against one's will. Qasarahu yaqsiruhu qasran and iqtasarahu mean he overpowered and subdued him. Qasarahu 'ala al-amr qasran means he compelled him to do the matter.
