ROOT حلبس   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is not attested in Al-Mufradāt; classical lexicography derives it from the semantic field of clinging, tenacious adherence and unyielding persistence, from which the meaning of courage and steadfastness is extrapolated.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is not attested; based on Lisān al-ʿArab, the ḥaqīqah is a person who clings firmly to something and does not let go, and by extension a brave, steadfast individual.
REC canon_def.ar     : الحَلْبَسُ والحَبَلْبَسُ والحُلابِسُ: الشجاع. والحَلْبَسُ: الحريض الملازم للشيء لا يفارقه.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ḥalbas, al-ḥabalbās, and al-ḥulābis: the brave one. And al-ḥalbas: the eager one who clings to something and does not leave it.
