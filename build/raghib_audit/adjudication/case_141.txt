REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root رفف is a subtle, rapid, and shimmering motion or vibration—a light quivering or flickering that manifests as a gleam, a flutter, or a delicate oscillation. It denotes the slightest perceptible movement or radiance, such as the shimmer of a polished surface, the quiver of a leaf, or the flutter of a wing, without any substantial displacement.
