REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root بله is a state of mental deficiency or heedlessness, specifically an incapacity to discern or manage evil (الغفلة عن الشرّ وعدم إحسانه). It denotes a congenital or acquired deficiency in practical intelligence, not a moral failing.
