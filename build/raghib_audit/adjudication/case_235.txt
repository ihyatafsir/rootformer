ROOT قردحم   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root denotes a specific, rugged, and remote topographical location—a place-name (موضع) characterized by harsh, broken terrain. Its foundational physical origin is the concrete designation of a desolate, stony ground or a rugged tract of land, from which the abstract sense of scattering and dispersal derives by analogy: to be cast into such broken country is to be dispersed beyond recovery.
REC haqiqah_literal  : A proper noun (اسم علم) for a specific rugged, stony place or tract of land. In its strict literal usage, it is a toponym, indeclinable (غير مصروف) as a place-name, denoting a harsh, broken terrain. The phrase ذهبوا شَعالِيل بِقِرْدَحْمةٍ means 'they scattered in every direction at Qirdahma,' i.e., they dispersed across a rugged, remote location.
