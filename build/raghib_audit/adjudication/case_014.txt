ROOT سحت   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the act of peeling, scraping, or eradicating something down to its core—specifically, stripping away the outer layer (qishr) or fat from flesh, or shaving hair to the root. This concrete sense of total removal and uprooting is the root's primary material meaning.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is: to shave hair completely during a haircut or trimming, to peel fat away from flesh, to scrape the surface of the earth, and in circumcision, to perform the procedure thoroughly and exhaustively until the flesh is worn down.
REC canon_def.ar     : السُّحْتُ والسُّحُتُ: كلُّ حرام قبيح الذِّكر؛ وقيل: هو ما خَبُثَ من المَكاسب وحَرُم فلَزِمَ عنه العارُ، وقَبيحُ الذِّكْر، كَثَمن الكلب والخمر والخنزير.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Suḥt and al-suḥut: every forbidden thing of vile repute; it is said to be any foul and unlawful earning that entails disgrace and infamy, such as the price of a dog, wine, or swine.
