REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is a sudden, forceful emission or gust — whether of wind, scent, or a striking limb — characterized by a single, discrete impulse rather than sustained duration.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the blowing or wafting of wind or fragrance in a single gust (nafḥah), and the striking or kicking of an animal with its hoof or a person with a sword.
REC canon_def.ar     : النَّفْحَةُ دُفْعَةُ الرِّيحِ، طَيِّبَةً كَانَتْ أَوْ خَبِيثَةً؛ وَلَهُ نَفْحَةٌ طَيِّبَةٌ. وَنَفَحَتِ الرِّيحُ: هَبَّتْ.
REC canon_def.en     : A nafḥah is a gust of wind, whether pleasant or foul; and it may be said that something has a pleasant nafḥah. The wind nafaḥa means it blew.
