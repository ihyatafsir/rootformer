REC ontological_core : The root ازج (ʾ-z-j) denotes the foundational physical act of joining, pairing, or bringing two things into close conjunction, with a secondary concrete sense of a narrow, constricted passage or a thing tightly bound.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the joining or pairing of two things, or a narrow, constricted place or passage; also used for a tightly bound or compressed object.

REC canon_def.en     : Al-azj: narrowness; also said to be the joining of two things; al-azaj: a narrow mountain pass.
