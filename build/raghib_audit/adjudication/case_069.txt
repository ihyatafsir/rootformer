ROOT جشر   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root is the act of taking livestock out to graze in the fresh spring pasture (al-jashar) near the dwellings, without returning them to the household for the night.
REC haqiqah_literal  : To send horses or livestock out to graze in the spring herbage (al-jashar) in front of the tents or houses, and to remain with them overnight in the pasture rather than returning to the family dwellings.
