REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the nasal passage and its internal anatomy (al-khayāshīm), and the condition of a man who is akhsham—one who cannot detect odors due to a blockage or defect in his nasal passages. The wind or odor is said to penetrate (tasawwara) into the khayāshīm, i.e., the olfactory recesses of the nose.
