ROOT حكر   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root حكر is the act of withholding, confining, or hoarding a commodity—specifically foodstuffs—by collecting and retaining it in expectation of scarcity and price inflation. It denotes a deliberate, self-serving retention that restricts circulation and access.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal meaning is the hoarding of food (احتكار الطعام), i.e., its retention and confinement for the purpose of selling at a higher price during times of scarcity. REC canon_def.ar     : الحَكْرُ: ادِّخارُ الطعام للتَّرَبُّصِ، وصاحبُه مُحْتَكِرٌ. والاحْتِكارُ: جمع الطعام ونحوه مما يؤكل واحتباسُه انتظارَ وقتِ الغَلاءِ به.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ḥakr is the storing of food in anticipation [of scarcity]; one who does so is a muḥtakir. Al-iḥtikār is the gathering of food and similar edible goods and retaining them in expectation of a time of high prices.
