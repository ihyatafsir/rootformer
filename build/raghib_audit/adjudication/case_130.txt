REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the act of overlaying, covering, or fitting one thing closely upon another, such that the two correspond and coincide — a concrete operation of physical matching and concealment.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is التَّطْبِيس (al-taṭbīs), the act of applying or fitting one thing over another (التَّطْبِيق), i.e., bringing two surfaces or entities into close correspondence and covering.
REC canon_def.ar     : التَّطْبِيسُ: التَّطْبِيقُ. والطَّبَسَان: كُورَتَانِ بِخُرَاسَانَ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-taṭbīs is the act of fitting or overlaying (al-taṭbīq). And al-Ṭabasān refers to two districts in Khurāsān.
