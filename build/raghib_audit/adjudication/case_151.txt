ROOT بعثق   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the sudden, forceful emergence and overflow of water from a breach or collapsed side of a basin or reservoir, i.e., the rupture of a containment structure and the consequent gushing forth of its liquid contents.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the outflow of water from a broken side or fissure of a basin (ḥawḍ) or a reservoir (jābiyah), and the verb tabaʿthaqa denotes the event of a side of such a structure breaking so that water pours out from it.
REC canon_def.ar     : البَعْثَقَةُ: خُروج الماء من غائِل حَوْضٍ أو جابِيةٍ. وتَبَعْثَقَ إذا انكسرت منه ناحية ففاضَ منها.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-baʿthaqah: the outflow of water from a breach in a basin or a reservoir. And tabaʿthaqa: when one side of it breaks so that water pours out from it.
