REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the emission and flow of moisture from the mouth or body of an animal, specifically saliva, foam, or dripping fluid, as attested in the classical lexical tradition.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is the flowing saliva or foam of a horse or beast, and the act of an animal lowering itself to urinate; also the anointing or moistening of the head with oil and of bread with clarified butter or condiment.
