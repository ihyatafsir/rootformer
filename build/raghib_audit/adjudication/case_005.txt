ROOT بيض   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root ب-ي-ض is the color white, understood as the luminous, unmixed, and manifest opposite of black, predicated of animals, plants, and all entities susceptible to coloration.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, sensory reality (Ḥaqīqah) of البياض is the color white itself, the quality of an object that is أبيض (white). It is a perceptible accident (ʿaraḍ) subsisting in bodies, and its verbal noun denotes the state or instance of being white.
