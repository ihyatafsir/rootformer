ROOT غيذ   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root غيذ denotes the act of forming a correct conjecture that subsequently proves true; its foundational physical origin is the hitting or striking of a target, from which the cognitive sense of 'hitting upon the truth' is derived.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is 'to strike or hit a target' (as in striking with a weapon or arrow), which then metaphorically extends to the mental act of forming an opinion that corresponds to reality.
REC canon_def.ar     : الغَيْذان: الذي يظن فيصيب
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ghaydhān: the one who conjectures and then hits upon the truth.
