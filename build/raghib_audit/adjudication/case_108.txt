ROOT هذم   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root هذم is the swift, forceful, and total severance or engulfing of a thing—an act of rapid cutting or swallowing that leaves no trace, as in the poet's image of the fiery abyss (al-lihab) consuming the horizons.
REC haqiqah_literal  : To cut something swiftly and completely; to engulf or obliterate a thing entirely, as a sword (sayf mihdham) that cuts through with speed and finality.
REC canon_def.ar     : هَذَمَ الشيءَ يَهْذِمه هَذْماً: غيّبه أَجمع؛ وسيف مِهْذَم وهِذَام: سريع القطع.
REC canon_def.en     : He cut the thing swiftly and completely, engulfing it entirely; and a sword that is mihdham or hidhām is one that cuts with speed and finality.
