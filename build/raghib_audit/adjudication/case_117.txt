Al-Jawharī restricts بِلِز to the pattern فِعِل (fiʿil) with kasrah on both the first and second radicals, meaning simply 'bulky.' REC canon_def.en     : A woman who is بِلِز or بِلِزّ: bulky and compactly fleshed. Thaʿlab said: only two adjectival forms occur on the pattern فِعِل — a woman بِلِز and a she-ass إِبِد. And a camel بَلَنْزى: thick and coarse.
