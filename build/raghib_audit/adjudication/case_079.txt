ROOT ذمر   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root ذمر is the act of combining blame or rebuke with urgent incitement—specifically, a leader's sharp censure of his troops to spur them into battle, or a shepherd's tactile stimulation of a newborn camel's neck to rouse it. The root thus denotes a forceful, often harsh, arousal that fuses reproach with motivational propulsion.

It is said: the commander yudhammiru (incites) his companions in war—he makes them hear what is disagreeable in order to sharpen them. And they are seen yatadhāmarūn (mutually inciting) in war.
