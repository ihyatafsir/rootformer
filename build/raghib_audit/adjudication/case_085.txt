ROOT صيح   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root ص-ي-ح is the emission of a loud, piercing sound, specifically a vocal cry that strains the utterer's maximum capacity. It denotes an intense auditory event, whether from a human, animal, or inanimate object, and by extension, the act of calling out to someone or something.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning is to shout, cry out, or emit a loud sound with one's utmost effort. It also encompasses calling out to someone (صاح به) and the reciprocal act of shouting at one another (تصايحوا). Additionally, it is used literally for the splitting or cracking of objects like a garment or a stick (انصاح الثوب, انصاحت العصا).
