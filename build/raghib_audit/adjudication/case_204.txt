ROOT صلخد   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the robust, mature, and powerful male camel, specifically denoting a stallion distinguished by advanced age, formidable strength, and imposing length; it is a quadriliteral root (rubāʿī) with no triliteral derivation, and its primary semantic nucleus is the concrete image of a mighty, seasoned beast of burden.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah (inferred from Lisān al-ʿArab) is: a mature, strong, and tall male camel; a stallion of advanced age and great power; also used for a strong stallion, with the feminine form denoting a she-camel of similar qualities.
