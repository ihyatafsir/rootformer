ROOT نزف   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the exhaustive drawing-off of a well's water, ladle by ladle, until the source is completely depleted; hence the root denotes total evacuation of a contained substance, whether water, blood, tears, or, by extension, the rational faculties.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is to drain a well completely by drawing its water out gradually, or for a well to become so drained; it is used transitively (nazafa al-bīra) and intransitively (nazafat al-bīru), and in the passive (nuzifat). It also applies literally to the complete extraction of blood (nazafa damahu) or tears (nazafa damʿahu).
REC canon_def.ar     : نَزَفْت ماء البئر نَزْفاً إذا نزحْته كله، ونزَفَت هي، يتعدّى ولا يتعدّى، ونُزِفت أَيضاً على ما لم يسم فاعله. ونزف دمه أو دمعه أي نزع كله. ومنه قيل: سكران نزيف: نزف فهمه بسكره.
