ROOT كرتح   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the violent bringing-down of a body to the ground, i.e. throwing someone down prostrate, together with a rapid, jolting forward motion in walking; the two senses share the primitive image of abrupt downward or forward displacement of the body.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is: كَرْتَحَه 'he threw him down, prostrated him, cast him to the ground'; and كَرْتَح في مشيه 'he hastened, moved swiftly in his walking'.
REC canon_def.ar     : كَرْتَحه: صَرَعَه. وكَرْتَح في مشيه: أَسرع.
REC canon_def.en     : Kartaḥahu: he threw him down, prostrated him. And kartaḥa fī mashyihi: he hastened in his walking.

ROOT كندش   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the designation of a specific bird, the magpie (Pica pica), a corvid known for its harsh chattering call and its reputation in classical Arabic lore as a creature of ill omen and innate malevolence.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the magpie (العَقْعَق), a black-and-white bird of the crow family, characterized by its noisy, chattering cry and its association with mischief and bad luck.
REC canon_def.ar     : الكُنْدُشُ: العَقْعَقُ. قال ابن الأَعرابي: أَخبرني المفضّل يقال هو أَخبَثُ من كُنْدُشٍ، وهو العَقْقعَق.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Kundush: the magpie. Ibn al-Aʿrābī said: Al-Mufaḍḍal informed me that it is said, 'He is more malignant than a kundush,' which is the magpie.

ROOT لحط   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root ل-ح-ط is the act of sprinkling or scattering water upon a surface, specifically a door or wall, as a practical measure of dust suppression, cooling, or ritual cleansing.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, physical meaning is to sprinkle water on the door of one's house, as attested in the report that ʿAlī passed by a group who were sprinkling water on the door of their dwelling.
REC canon_def.ar     : اللَّحْطُ: الرَّشُّ. يقال: لَحَطَ بابَ دارِه إِذا رَشَّه بالماء.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Laḥṭ is the sprinkling of water. It is said: he sprinkled water on the door of his house.

ROOT لشش   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root لشش denotes lightness, agility, and rapid, unencumbered motion. Its foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the quality of being light in weight or movement, often applied to a person's constitution or gait. It is a rare root in Arabic, with the sequence of ش after ل being uncommon, as noted by classical philologists.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is 'lightness' or 'agility' in a person, as in رجل لَشْلاشٌ (a light, agile man). It also denotes the rapid, repeated movement or trembling associated with lightness, as in اللَّشْلَشَةُ (frequent, quick motion).
