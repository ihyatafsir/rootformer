REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the involuntary or deliberate utterance of the interjection 'āwh/awwāh' (أوه), a vocalization expressing pain, grief, or intense emotional distress, rooted in the primal human sound of lamentation.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the act of saying 'āwh āwh' (أوه أوه) — a repeated sigh or groan — and any speech that signifies grief or sorrow; this is termed taʾawwuh.

ROOT بيض   stratum=MA
