ROOT لشش   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root لشش denotes lightness, agility, and rapid, unencumbered motion. Its foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the quality of being light in weight or movement, often applied to a person's constitution or gait. It is a rare root in Arabic, with the sequence of ش after ل being uncommon, as noted by classical philologists.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is 'lightness' or 'agility' in a person, as in رجل لَشْلاشٌ (a light, agile man). It also denotes the rapid, repeated movement or trembling associated with lightness, as in اللَّشْلَشَةُ (frequent, quick motion).
