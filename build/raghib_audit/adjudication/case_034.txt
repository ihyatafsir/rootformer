REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the reciprocal transaction of exchange: the transfer of a commodity against a price, such that one party surrenders the object and receives the price while the other surrenders the price and receives the object. The root's primary semantic nucleus is the act of exchanging one entity for another, whether by sale or purchase.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the act of buying and selling, i.e., the exchange of a commodity for a price. In this literal sense, the buyer (al-mushtarī) pays the price and takes the commodity, while the seller (al-bāʾiʿ) delivers the commodity and takes the price.
