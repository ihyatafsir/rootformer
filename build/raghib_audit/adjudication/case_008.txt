ROOT حوب   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the sharp cry or call used to restrain and drive back camels (ḥawb as a deterrent cry), from which the root's semantic nucleus of restraint, prohibition, and consequent moral liability is derived.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the act of crying out to camels to restrain them (ḥāba ḥawban), and by extension, the state of being restrained or held back from something; also, the concrete kinship obligation or right (ḥawbah) that binds one to protect and provide for weak dependents, and the physical need or destitution (ḥawbah) that drives one to transgression.
REC canon_def.ar     : الحَوْبُ: الإِثْمُ. والحَوْبَةُ: الحاجَةُ والمَسْكَنَةُ، والحُرْمَةُ والحَقُّ، والقَرابَةُ من قِبَلِ الأُمِّ. والحَوْباءُ: النَّفْسُ. والأَصْلُ فيه حَوْبٌ لِزَجْرِ الإِبِلِ.
REC canon_def.en     : Ḥawb is sin. Ḥawbah is need and destitution, an inviolable right and obligation, and maternal kinship. Ḥawbāʾ is the soul. Its origin is the cry 'ḥawb' used to restrain camels.
