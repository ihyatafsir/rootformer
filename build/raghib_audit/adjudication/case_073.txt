ROOT خبا   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin centers on concealment, hiding, and the act of rendering something invisible or sheltered from view. It encompasses both the physical act of covering or storing something away and the structural notion of a shelter (خِباء) that veils its occupants.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the act of hiding, concealing, or storing something away (خبأ), and the physical structure of a tent made of wool or hair (خِباء) that provides concealment. It also includes the literal sense of a young woman kept secluded (مخبأة) and the vessel for storing vinegar (خابية).
REC canon_def.ar     : الخِباءُ من الأَبنية: واحد الأَخْبية، وهو ما كان من وَبَر أَو صوف ولا يكون من شَعَر، وهو على عمودين أَو ثلاثة، وما فوقَ ذلك فهو بَيْت. والخبء: ما خفي، ومنه: والله يخرج الخبء.
