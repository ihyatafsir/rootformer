ROOT حرذن   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is a small, low-creeping creature — a lizard (ʿaẓāʾah) — and, by extension, a beast of burden ridden to the point of total exhaustion, i.e., a quadruped reduced to its last remnant of strength.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah (as delineated from Lisān al-ʿArab) is: a small lizard (ʿaẓāʾah), specifically the ḥirzaw n, a creeping reptile; and, in the domain of camels, a riding camel that is mounted continuously until no strength remains in it.
