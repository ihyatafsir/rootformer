ROOT هكر   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the state of being overwhelmed by astonishment — a cognitive-affective rupture in which the mind is seized by an object or event that exceeds its ordinary categories of expectation and comprehension.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal, non-figurative meaning is intense wonder or astonishment (al-ʿajab al-shadīd); the verbal noun denotes the sheer magnitude of amazement, and the derived verb describes a person whose astonishment has become acute and overpowering.
REC canon_def.ar     : الهَكْرُ: العَجَبُ، وقيل: الهَكْرُ أَشدُّ العجبِ. هَكِرَ يَهْكَرُ هَكَراً وهِكْراً، فهو هَكِرٌ: اشتدَّ عَجَبُه.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-hakr is astonishment; it is also said that al-hakr is the most intense form of astonishment. He was astonished, being astonished with astonishment and intense wonder, so he is wonder-struck: his astonishment became acute.
