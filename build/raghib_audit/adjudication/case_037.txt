ROOT طول   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is spatial extension in a single dimension — the quality of a body, object, or duration being drawn out in length, standing in direct opposition (نقيض) to shortness (قِصَر). This primary corporeal sense of linear elongation is the root from which all temporal, quantitative, and moral extensions are derived.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the contrary of shortness (نقيض القِصَر) in people and other beings, whether animate or inanimate: the state of a thing being extended in length. It applies to concrete entities (aʿyān) such as a body, a rope, or a horse's tether, and to the act of lengthening or letting out (e.g., طَوِّلْ فَرَسَكَ, 'let out your horse's tether').
REC canon_def.ar     : الطُّولُ: نقيض القِصَر في الناس وغيرهم من الحيوان والمَوات؛ ويقال للشيء الطويل: طالَ يَطُولُ طُولاً، فهو طَويلٌ وطُوالٌ. والطَّوْلُ: الفضل والمن والسعة والقدرة.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ṭūl is the contrary of shortness (al-qiṣar) in people and other beings, whether animate or inanimate; a long thing is said to be ṭāla yaṭūlu ṭūlan, and it is ṭawīl or ṭuwāl. Al-ṭawl denotes favor, beneficence, ample means, and capacity.
