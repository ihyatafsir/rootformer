ROOT قهقم   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on massive, engulfing bulk — specifically the image of a creature or beast of enormous size that swallows and devours everything, and by extension the towering, raging male camel (al-faḥl al-ḍakhm al-mughtalim). The quadriliteral reduplicative form (القَهْقَمّ) itself iconically reinforces the sense of heaving, repetitive engulfment.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, per Lisān al-ʿArab, is: a being or beast that swallows everything (al-ladhī yabtaliʿu kulla shayʾ); and a huge, raging stallion-camel (al-faḥl al-ḍakhm al-mughtalim). Abū ʿAmr records القَهْقَبّ and القَهْقَمّ as synonyms for the enormous camel (al-jamal al-ḍakhm).
REC canon_def.ar     : القِهْقَمُّ: الذي يبتلع كل شيء. والقَهْقَمُ: الفحل الضخم المغتلم. وقال أبو عمرو: القَهْقَبُّ والقَهْقَمُّ: الجمل الضخم.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-qihqamm: the one who swallows everything. Al-qahqam: the huge, raging stallion-camel. Abū ʿAmr said: al-qahqabb and al-qahqamm: the enormous camel.
