ROOT ضمي   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin is not attested in the classical lexical tradition as an independent primary root; it is identified by Ibn Manẓūr, on the authority of Thaʿlab from Ibn al-Aʿrābī, as a metathesized variant (maqlūb) of the root ضيم / ضام, whose concrete sense is to wrong, oppress, or diminish another's right.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is not established as an autonomous ḥaqīqah in Asās al-Balāghah; the form ضَمَى is recorded in Lisān al-ʿArab as a dialectal or transposed usage meaning 'he acted unjustly' (ظَلَمَ), standing in place of the standard ضَامَ.
REC canon_def.ar     : ضَمَى إِذا ظَلَمَ، كأَنَّهُ مَقْلُوبٌ مِنْ ضَامَ.
REC canon_def.en     : ḍamā means 'he acted unjustly'; it appears to be a metathesized form of ḍāma.
