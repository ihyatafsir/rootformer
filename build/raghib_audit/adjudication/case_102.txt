ROOT نشع   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the act of administering a substance into the mouth or throat, specifically forcing a liquid or medicine into someone's mouth, and by extension, the giving of a fee or bribe to a soothsayer to induce speech.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is: 'نشع الصبي الدواء وأنشعه' — he poured the medicine into the boy's mouth, or made him swallow it; the medicine itself is called 'نشوع', and the act is 'انتشعه'. The instrument is 'منشع الصبي' — the device used to administer medicine to the boy, i.e., a syringe or funnel.
REC canon_def.ar     : النَّشْعُ: جُعْلُ الكاهِنِ، وقد أَنْشَعَه. ونَشَعَ الصبي الدواء وأنشعه: أوجره وهو النشوع فانتشعه. وهذا منشع الصبي: لمسعطه.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Nashʿ: the soothsayer's fee, and he gave it to him. He administered medicine to the boy, or made him swallow it; the medicine is called nashūʿ, and the act is intashʿa. This is the boy's minshaʿ: a syringe or funnel.
