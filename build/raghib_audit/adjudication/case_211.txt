ROOT طرخ   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : A constructed basin or reservoir, wide like a cistern, positioned at the outlet of a water channel, in which water gathers before bursting forth to irrigate a cultivated field.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The concrete, physical referent is a wide, basin-like reservoir (mājil) built at the mouth of a canal or qanāt, functioning as a collecting and discharge point for irrigation water before it flows into the farmland.
REC canon_def.ar     : الطَّرْخَة: ماجِلٌ يُتَّخَذُ كالحوض الواسع عند مخرج القناة، يجتمع فيها الماء ثم يتفجر منها إلى المزرعة، وهو دخيل ليست فارسية محضة ولا عربية محضة.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-ṭarkhah: a wide basin-like reservoir constructed at the outlet of a water channel, in which water gathers and then bursts forth to the cultivated field; it is a loanword, neither purely Persian nor purely Arabic.
