ROOT سمندل   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root denotes a legendary creature—avian or reptilian—endowed with the property of self-renewal through fire: when its generative capacity ceases and it succumbs to senescence, it casts itself into live embers and is restored to youthful vigor, remaining incombustible.
REC haqiqah_literal  : A bird (ṭāʾir) that, upon the cessation of its progeny and the onset of decrepitude, throws itself into burning coals and thereby returns to its youthful state; alternatively, a creature (dābba) that enters fire without being consumed by it.
REC canon_def.ar     : السَّمَنْدَلُ: طائرٌ إِذا انقطع نَسْلُهُ وهَرِمَ أَلْقى نفسه في الجَمْر فيعود إِلى شَبابه، وقيل: دابَّةٌ يدخل النار فلا تُحْرِقه.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-samandal: a bird that, when its progeny ceases and it becomes decrepit, casts itself into live embers and returns to its youth; alternatively, a creature that enters fire and is not burned by it.
