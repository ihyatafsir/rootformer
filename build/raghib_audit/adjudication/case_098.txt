ROOT مهر   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin centers on the act of giving or assigning a dower (ṣadāq) to a woman, and by extension, the notion of a contractual payment that seals a union; from this concrete transactional sense, the root branches into the idea of skill or mastery, conceived as a 'price' or 'acquisition' of proficiency, and into the concrete noun for a camel's nose-ring (mihār), a device that controls and directs.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the dower given to a woman (mahr al-marʾah: to give her the dower), and the verbal noun denotes the act of assigning or paying that dower; it also literally denotes a nose-ring (mihār) placed in the nose of a camel, and a horse possessing such a ring (faras mumhar).
REC canon_def.ar     : المَهْرُ: الصَّداق، والجمع مُهور؛ وقد مَهَرَ المرأَةَ يَمْهَرُها ويَمْهُرُها مَهْراً وأَمْهَرَها. والمَاهِرُ: الحاذق بالشيء، وقد مَهَرَ يَمْهَرُ مَهَارَةً. والمِهَارُ: عود في أنف البختي.
