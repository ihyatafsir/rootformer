ROOT حظل   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the act of restraining, hindering, or preventing a being from acting or moving — a privative condition imposed upon a subject, conceived as a blockage or confinement of its capacity for free disposal and locomotion.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the prevention or hindrance of a person or animal from acting, moving, or disposing of affairs freely; it denotes a state of being held back, confined, or rendered incapable of motion or independent action.
REC canon_def.ar     : الحَظْل: المَنْع من التصرّف والحركةِ، حَظَل يَحْظِل ويَحْظُل حَظْلاً وحِظْلاناً وحَظَلاناً.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Ḥaẓl: prevention from free disposal and movement; ḥaẓala (yaḥẓil, yaḥẓul) — ḥaẓlan, ḥiẓlān, and ḥaẓalān.
