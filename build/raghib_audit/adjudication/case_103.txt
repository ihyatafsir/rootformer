ROOT نضح   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root نضح is the act of sprinkling or scattering water or a similar fluid in fine, dispersed droplets upon a surface, as opposed to a full, drenching pour. This core image underlies all subsequent literal and figurative applications.
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal meaning is the sprinkling of water upon something (نضح عليه الماء) or the sprinkling of a house with water (نضح البيت بالماء), which is characterized as رش (rashsh), i.e., a light, scattered sprinkling. It also literally denotes the oozing or exuding of sweat from the skin (نضح جلده بالعرق).
REC canon_def.ar     : النَّضْحُ: الرَّشُّ. نَضَحَ عَلَيْهِ المَاءَ يَنْضَحُهُ نَضْحًا، وَنَضَحَ البَيْتَ بِالمَاءِ، وَنَضَحَ جِلْدُهُ بِالعَرَقِ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Naḍḥ is sprinkling. One sprinkles water upon something (naḍaḥa ʿalayhi al-māʾa yanḍaḥuhu naḍḥan); one sprinkles the house with water; and one's skin exudes sweat.
