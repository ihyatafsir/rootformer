ROOT ششا   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : A marginal, quasi-nominal root attested only through the lexicographical report of Thaʿlab from Ibn al-Aʿrābī; its foundational physical referent is the shīṣ, the dry, hard, unpalatable date that remains on the palm cluster after the choice fruit has been taken.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The concrete, literal sense is the شِيص: the inferior, dried, inedible dates left behind on the raceme, i.e., the residual fruit of poor quality.
REC canon_def.ar     : الشَّشا: الشِّيصُ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-shashā: the shīṣ, i.e., the dry, inferior dates remaining on the raceme.
