ROOT نبه   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the abrupt rousing of a dormant or heedless being into wakefulness and attentive presence — the transition from the inertia of sleep or negligence to active consciousness and alertness.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, per Asās al-Balāghah, is awakening from sleep: a person wakes from his sleep (انتبه من نومه), is roused (استنبه، تنبّه), and one rouses him (نبّهه). It also denotes a person of alert, discerning mind (رجل نبيه), and to raise someone's name in renown (نبّهت باسمه: I made his name prominent).
REC canon_def.ar     : النُّبْهُ: القيامُ والانْتِباهُ من النوم، وقد نَبَّهَهُ وأَنْبَهَهُ من النوم فتَنَبَّهَ وانْتَبَهَ، وانْتَبَهَ من نومه: استَيقَظ.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-Nubh is rising and awakening from sleep; one rouses him (nabbahahu) or awakens him (anbahahu) from sleep, so he becomes roused (tanabbaha) and wakes (intabaha); he woke from his sleep: he became awake.
