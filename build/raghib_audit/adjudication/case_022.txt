ROOT قنع   stratum=MA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the act of inclining or bending toward something, specifically the lowering or turning of the head or hand toward an object, as when a camel lowers its head to drink or a person extends a hand in supplication. This concrete image of orientation toward a source underlies all subsequent semantic developments.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the act of bending or inclining the head, hand, or a vessel toward something, such as a camel lowering its head to a watering trough, a man extending his hands in prayer, or tilting a container to receive water from a stream. It also denotes the physical posture of a mouth with teeth inclined inward.
