ROOT كدا   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the retardation, delay, or sluggishness of a natural process — specifically, the tardiness of the earth in bringing forth its vegetation, such that the soil remains barren or slow to sprout despite its latent fertility.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, per Lisān al-ʿArab, is the delayed or sluggish emergence of vegetation from the earth: كَدَت الأَرض تَكْدو كَدْواً وكُدُوًّا, فهي كاديةٌ — 'The earth was slow to vegetate, hence it is kādiyah (sluggish/barren in sprouting).' The concrete referent is a tract of land whose herbage is late in appearing.
REC canon_def.ar     : كَدَت الأَرض تَكْدو كَدْواً وكُدُوًّا، فهي كاديةٌ إِذا أَبطأَ نباتها.
REC canon_def.en     : The earth was slow to vegetate with a kadw or kudūw, so it is kādiyah when its vegetation is tardy.
