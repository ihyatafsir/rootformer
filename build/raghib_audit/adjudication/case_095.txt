ROOT لحص   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root ل ح ص is the constriction and closure of a narrow aperture, specifically the eye of a needle, such that passage through it is blocked. The root thus denotes the state of being tightly closed, narrow, or impenetrable.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, as attested in Asās al-Balāghah, is the closing or sealing of the eye of a needle (خَرْتُ الإبرة), i.e., its becoming blocked and impassable.
REC canon_def.ar     : اللَّحْصُ واللَّحَصُ واللَّحِيصُ: الضَّيِّقُ. والتحص خرت الإبرة: انسد.
REC canon_def.en     : Al-laḥṣ, al-laḥaṣ, and al-laḥīṣ: the narrow. And the closing of the eye of a needle: it became blocked.
