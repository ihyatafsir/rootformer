REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the notion of a wedding celebration (ʿurs) and the bride (ʿarūs), from which derive associated meanings of adornment, intimacy, and habitation. A secondary, distinct root sense denotes bewilderment or stupefaction (dahash), as in ʿarisa al-rajul (the man was bewildered).
REC haqiqah_literal  : According to Asās al-Balāghah, the literal physical meaning is the wedding feast or celebration (ʿurs), and the bride (ʿarūs). It also literally denotes the act of a man endearing himself to his wife (tataʿarrasa li-mraʾatihi), and the lair or den of a lion (ʿirs al-asad) where it resides with its lioness (ʿarīs).
