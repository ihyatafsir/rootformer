ROOT خلا   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is spatial and temporal emptiness: a place or thing devoid of any occupant, structure, or content, hence 'to be empty, vacant, unoccupied.'
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the state of a place or thing being empty and devoid of any being or object, as in 'خلا المكان' (the place became empty) and 'الخلاء' (the open, unsheltered void).
REC canon_def.ar     : خَلا المكانُ والشيءُ يَخْلُو خُلُوّاً وخَلاءً وأَخْلَى إِذا لم يكن فيه أَحد ولا شيء فيه، وهو خالٍ. والخَلاءُ من الأَرض: قَرارٌ خالٍ. واسْتَخْلَى: كخَلا.
REC canon_def.en     : A place or thing becomes empty (خلا) with the verbal noun خُلُوّ and خَلاء, and one may say أَخْلَى when there is no one and nothing in it; it is then خالٍ (empty). الخَلاء from the earth is a vacant, level tract. اِسْتَخْلَى is synonymous  […]
