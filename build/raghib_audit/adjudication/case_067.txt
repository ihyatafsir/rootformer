ROOT بنق   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) centers on the act of gathering, tightening, and securing disparate elements into a coherent, bounded whole — specifically, the stitching or fastening of garment seams and the binding of a book's quires.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah: the banāʾiq are the seams or stitched joins of a wide shirt (qamīṣ wāsiʿ al-banāʾiq), also described as its dakhārīṣ (seam-lines) or its lining (libn). To banāʾiq a garment is to sew its seams; to banāʾiq a book is to bind or stitch its quires together.
REC canon_def.ar     : بَنَّقَ الكِتَابَ: لُغَةٌ فِي نَبَّقَهُ. وَبَنَّقَ كَلَامَهُ: جَمَعَهُ وَسَوَّاهُ، وَمِنْهُ بَنَائِقُ القَمِيصِ أَيْ جَمْعُ شَيْءٍ. وَالبَنِيقَةُ: خِيَاطَةُ القَمِيصِ، وَالجَمْعُ بَنَائِقُ.
