ROOT ميث   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root م-ي-ث is the act of dissolving a solid substance—such as salt, bread, or clay—in water through kneading, rubbing, and maceration until it loses its solid form and becomes fully incorporated into the liquid medium.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is twofold: (1) the softening and leveling of land, as in أرض ميثاء (soft, level ground) and أراض ميث (soft, level lands); and (2) the physical dissolution of a solid in water, as in ماث الخبز والملح والطين في الماء (he dissolved bread, salt, and clay in water) and انماث (it dissolved).
REC canon_def.ar     : مَاثَ الشيءَ مَيْثاً: مَرَسَه. وماثَ المِلحَ في الماء: أَذابه؛ وكذلك الطين، وقد انماثَ. وكلُّ شيءٍ مَرَسته في الماء فذاب فيه، فقد مِثْته ومُثْته.
