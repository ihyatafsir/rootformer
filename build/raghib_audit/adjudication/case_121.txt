ROOT حثر   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root ح-ث-ر is a granular, rough, or crusted condition affecting a surface, specifically the eye, arising from accreted discharge or eruptive pustules.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is a roughness or granulation experienced in the eye due to rheum (ramas), or the emergence of a red pustule or blister (bathr) on the eyelids, accompanied by a scaly or crusted texture.
