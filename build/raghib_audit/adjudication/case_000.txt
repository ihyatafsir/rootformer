REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) of the root is the bodily organ of hearing — the ear — conceived as the locus of auditory apprehension. From this concrete anatomical nucleus, the root radiates outward to denote the act of listening, the granting of permission (since one who is heard is thereby admitted), and the act of proclamation or warning (since a summons is issued to be heard).
