ROOT حنق   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the constriction and compression of the belly against the spine, as seen in a lean, emaciated horse; from this concrete image of tight inward contraction, the root extends to the psychological constriction of intense inward rage.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the adhesion of the belly to the back in a horse or other animal due to emaciation and leanness, as in the verse of Labīd: 'by a travel-worn steed, whose belly clung to its spine and hump.' It also denotes the compact, gathered head of grain before its awns and kernels emerge.
REC canon_def.ar     : الحَنَقُ: شِدَّةُ الاغْتِيَاظِ. وأَحْنَقَ الفرسُ وغيرهُ إذا التصقَ بطنهُ بصلبهِ ضُمْرًا.
REC canon_def.en     : Ḥanaq is intense wrath. Aḥnaqa al-faras or other animal means its belly adhered to its spine due to emaciation.
