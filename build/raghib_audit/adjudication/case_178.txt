ROOT دفس   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root denotes the onset of blackness or dark discoloration upon a surface, specifically the face, without prior cause or illness; its foundational physical image is that of a darkening or staining that overtakes a visage.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the blackening of the face without any underlying disease or external cause, as attested in the verbal form أَدْفَسَ.
REC canon_def.ar     : أَدْفَسَ الرجلُ إِذا اسودَّ وجهه من غير علة
REC canon_def.en     : A man's face turned black without any illness or cause.
