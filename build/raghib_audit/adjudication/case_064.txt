ROOT ازم   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root أزم is the act of biting down hard and holding fast with the teeth, specifically the clamping of the horse's mouth onto the bit of the bridle, and by extension any firm, tenacious grip or constriction.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is the biting and holding fast of a horse upon the iron bit of the bridle (فأس اللجام), such that it clenches it firmly; hence a horse so described is أزوم. It also denotes the seizing and holding of property or wealth, as in أخذ مالي فأزم عليه.
REC canon_def.ar     : أزم الفرس على فأس اللجام: عض عليه وأمسكه، وفرس أزوم. وأخذ مالي فأزم عليه. ومنه قيل للحمية الأزم. ورجل أزوم: قليل الرزء من الطعام.
REC canon_def.en     : The horse bit down on the iron bit of the bridle and held it fast; hence a horse is أزوم. He seized my wealth and held it firmly. From this, self-restraint in diet is called الأزم. A man who is أزوم eats little and causes scant loss to food […]
