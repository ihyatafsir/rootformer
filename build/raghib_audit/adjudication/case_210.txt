ROOT ضوم   stratum=ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is not attested in the classical lexicographical tradition as an independent radical cluster; Ibn Manẓūr records it only as a dialectal or orthographic variant of ضيم (ḍ-y-m), whose primary physical sense is the exertion of force or pressure upon a thing, resulting in injustice or wrong.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning, as recorded by Ibn Manẓūr, is the act of wronging or oppressing someone: ضُمْتُه, i.e., ظَلَمْتُه ('I wronged him'). This is treated as a variant of ضِمْتُه, with the same concrete sense of imposing an unjust burden or harm.
REC canon_def.ar     : ضُمْتُه: كضِمْتُه، أَي ظَلَمْتُه.
REC canon_def.en     : I wronged him: equivalent to ḍimtuhu, meaning I treated him unjustly.
