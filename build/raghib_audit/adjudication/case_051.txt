ROOT زحح   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : The root's foundational physical origin (Aṣl al-Wadʿ) is the forcible, hasty displacement of a thing from its place—a swift pulling or shoving that removes an entity from its locus.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning is to pull or shove something in haste, thereby removing it from its position; the reduplicated form زُحْزِحَ denotes being moved away and distanced.
REC canon_def.ar     : زُحْزِحَ أَي نُحِّيَ وبُعِّدَ. وزَحَّ الشيءَ يَزُحُّه زَحّاً: جذبه في عَجَلَة.
REC canon_def.en     : زُحْزِحَ means he was removed and distanced. And زَحَّ الشيءَ يَزُحُّه زَحّاً: he pulled it in haste.
