ROOT حيث   stratum=Ma
REC ontological_core : An indeterminate locative particle (ẓarf mubham) denoting an unspecified place, whose precise reference is disambiguated only by the clause that follows it.
REC haqiqah_literal  : A literal, indeterminate adverb of place (ẓarf makān mubham) governing the spatial sense of 'where' or 'in the place that', whose referent is specified by the subsequent clause.
REC canon_def.ar     : حَيْثُ: ظَرْفٌ مُبْهَمٌ مِنَ الأَمْكِنَةِ، مَضْمُومٌ، وَبَعْضُ العَرَبِ يَفْتَحُهُ، وَزَعَمُوا أَنَّ أَصْلَهَا الوَاوُ؛ وَإِنَّمَا قُلِبَتِ الوَاوُ يَاءً طَلَبَ الخِفَّةِ.
REC canon_def.en     : Ḥaythu is an indeterminate locative adverb of place, pronounced with a ḍammah, though some Arab speakers pronounce it with a fatḥah; it is claimed that its original root was wāw, which was changed to yāʾ for the sake of phonetic lightness.
