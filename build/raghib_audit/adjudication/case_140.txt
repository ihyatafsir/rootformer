ROOT رصص   stratum=mA
REC ontological_core : The foundational physical origin of the root رصص is the act of tightly joining, compacting, and aligning solid discrete units—such as stones, bricks, or teeth—into a single cohesive, interlocked structure, eliminating gaps and ensuring firm mutual support.
REC haqiqah_literal  : The literal physical meaning according to Asās al-Balāghah is the construction of a tightly packed, well-aligned building (بنيان مرصوص ومرصص), the compacting of stones (ارتصت الجنادل وترصصت), the close alignment of teeth (في أسنانه رصص), and the act of a hen or ostrich gathering and arranging its eggs with beak and feet (رصت الدجاجة والنعامة بيضها).
REC canon_def.ar     : رَصَّ البُنْيَانَ يَرُصُّهُ رَصًّا، فَهُوَ مَرْصُوصٌ وَرَصِيصٌ، وَرَصَّصَهُ: أَحْكَمَهُ وَجَمَعَهُ وَضَمَّ بَعْضَهُ إِلَى بَعْضٍ. وَكُلُّ مَا أُحْكِمَ وَضُمَّ فَقَدْ رُصَّ.
REC canon_def.en     : He compacted the building, joining it tightly; it is marṣūṣ and raṣīṣ. He made it tight: he perfected it, gathered it, and joined its parts to one another. Everything that is made firm and joined together is said to be raṣṣed.
